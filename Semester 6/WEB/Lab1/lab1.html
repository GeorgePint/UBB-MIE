The Dawn of Digital Entertainment

Video games have evolved from simple electronic amusements into a multi-billion dollar global industry. In the early days, pioneers created basic games like Tennis for Two and Spacewar! on massive, room-sized mainframe computers.
However, it was the release of arcade cabinets and home consoles in the 1970s that truly brought gaming to the masses.

Iconic Milestones

  • 1958: Creation of Tennis for Two
  • 1972: Magnavox Odyssey (First Home Console)
  • 1985: Super Mario Bros. revitalizes the home console industry
